## Reconciliation and facts to review

- **Ego-Pi:** The CV specifies **CVPR Findings, 2026**. The selected-work homepage says only CVPR 2026, and its old bibliography says under review 2025. The site uses CVPR Findings. The CV's author list includes S. Liu and C. Gao, whereas the actual paper lists Sirui Chen and Cong Zhao. The arXiv author order (Kim, Wang, Fu, Chen, Zhao, Lai, Finn) is used; it agrees with the project's BibTeX, though the project's visible author row swaps Fu and Chen. The project marks Kim and Wang as equal contributors.
- **Under-review records:** Open-H-Embodiment and the soft robotic guidewire paper are presented as preprints with an explicit “Under review” venue. This avoids implying acceptance from their placement in the CV's journal/conference sections. Open-H's year is confirmed as 2026 by arXiv. RSS 2023 micromanipulation is a workshop paper in the preprint/report category, following the CV's technical-report grouping.
- **Digital twin paper:** The CV describes the original 2024 technical report. Its arXiv v2 changes the title and expands the author list, and a 2025 workshop program also contains a related title. The entry intentionally links v1 and preserves the CV's original title, seven-author list, year, and report status, rather than silently merging distinct versions.
- **CoRL eye surgery date:** The CV uses 2021, which matches the PMLR publication year. PMLR calls the proceedings “2020 Conference on Robot Learning.” The displayed record uses the publication year 2021 without embedding a conference year in the venue.
- **Author display:** The owner's name is normalized to “Ji Woong Kim” across entries so it can be emphasized consistently. Original sources also use “Ji Woong (Brian) Kim” and “Ji Woong Brian Kim.” Other authors are expanded only when primary sources support the spelling; otherwise the CV's initials remain. No invented DOIs, authors, or BibTeX are included.
- **News dates:** The homepage dates the Yale symposium May 2026, while the CV says June 2026. It dates the IROS panel October 2026, while the CV lists a surgical-autonomy panel in September 2026. The news data preserves the homepage dates; the owner should reconcile these two items when updating news. No exact day was inferred. The additional upcoming talks present only in the CV are not inserted as invented news announcements.
- **Press claims:** SRT-H's July 2025 cover, 2nd-highest Altmetric ranking, and “more than 280 news articles” claim are preserved in the dated July 2025 news announcement at the owner's request. They represent the owner's historical announcement, not an independently verified present-day ranking or article count. The ranking retains the original Altmetric link and also appears with July 2025 context in the research feature. SRT's oral designation and 4.5% figure come from the CV/homepage.
- **News links:** Historical links are retained where useful. The GenAI4Health homepage now redirects to its 2026 event, so the 2025 update has no potentially misleading link. CRSA is linked to the 2025 event page; the copy omits the homepage's “Los Angeles” location because the event page gives Huntington Beach.
- **Cooperative eye control:** Russell H. Taylor appears in the paper's author list but is absent from the CV's abbreviated list. The website follows the paper and includes him.
- **Missing paper links:** Two older conference entries have no individually verified paper URL yet (region-targeted guidance and chance-constraint retinal surgery). They remain complete bibliographic entries. Add publisher URLs when verified; do not substitute guessed links.

The site data is an editable migration of the owner's academic record, not an independent claim that every listed acceptance or invitation was re-confirmed with the event organizer.
